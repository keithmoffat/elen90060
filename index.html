Power System Analysis

I teach ELEN90060 Power System Analysis in the Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering at the University of Melbourne.

The purpose of the course is to understand complex grid phenomena from a handful of fundamental concepts.

The course material takes a first-principles approach, focusing on simple examples.
