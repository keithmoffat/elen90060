ELEN90060 Lecture slides

Power System Analysis

Keith Moffat · Senior Lecturer, University of Melbourne

I teach this subject in the Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering. We cover power flow in transmission and distribution networks, fault analysis and protection, power system dynamics, frequency and voltage control, voltage collapse, transient and small-signal stability, and power system security.
